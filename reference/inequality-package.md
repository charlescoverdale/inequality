# inequality: Inequality Measurement, Decomposition, and Poverty Analysis

Tools for measuring income and wealth inequality. Computes the Gini
coefficient with bootstrap or asymptotic confidence intervals following
Davidson (2009)
[doi:10.1016/j.jeconom.2008.09.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2008.09.011)
, the extended S-Gini family, Theil T and L indices (generalised entropy
family), the Atkinson index, the Kolm absolute inequality index, Palma
ratio, Hoover index, percentile ratios, and Lorenz curves. Supports
between-within group decomposition following Bourguignon (1979)
[doi:10.2307/1914138](https://doi.org/10.2307/1914138) , income share
tabulation, concentration indices for health inequality with Erreygers
(2009) correction, Kakwani tax progressivity and Reynolds-Smolensky
redistribution indices, Foster-Greer-Thorbecke poverty measures
including the Sen index, growth incidence curves following Ravallion and
Chen (2003)
[doi:10.1016/S0165-1765(02)00205-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0165-1765%2802%2900205-7)
, and Wolfson polarisation. All functions accept optional survey weights
and work with data from any source.
